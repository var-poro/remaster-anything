# Legal ground rules

Not legal advice. These are the project's rules, chosen to keep it clearly on the side of
modding for people who own the game.

- The repo contains no game files and no assets derived from them, including upscaled
  textures: those stay derived works of the original.
- Every player works from a copy they own and generates assets locally.
- Third-party mods are linked or downloaded from their official source, per their license.
  If redistribution is unclear or forbidden, link only.
- Small before/after crops for documentation are the only game imagery in the repo.
- Single-player only: nothing touching anti-cheat or online services.
- Credit every project you build on.
